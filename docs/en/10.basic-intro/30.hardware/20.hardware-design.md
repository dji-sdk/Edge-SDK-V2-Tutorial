This section introduces the hardware design constraints for connecting a third-party dock to the O4 Ground Station, including wireless charging structural constraints, thermal dissipation constraints, ACDC module specifications, and flight system requirements. When designing dock hardware, manufacturers must meet the following requirements to ensure charging efficiency, thermal safety, and normal drone takeoff and landing.

:::warning Note:
FlightHub 2 (DJI's enterprise cloud platform) does not yet support the visualization and management of third-party docks.
:::

## Wireless Charging Module

The wireless charging module is provided by DJI and consists of the TX coil on the dock side, the RX coil on the drone side, and the ACDC module. Wireless charging is completed by coupling the TX coil with the RX coil, and the ACDC module supplies power for wireless charging and the dock load. To ensure charging efficiency and safety, the following structural and hardware constraints must be met.

### Structural Constraints

- **Coil Spacing**: The vertical distance between the TX coil and the RX coil is 8 ± 1.5 mm (the thickness of the medium between the two coils, i.e., the distance from the bottom surface of the RX coil to the top surface of the TX coil), and the horizontal distance is 0–5 mm. The optimal operating point of the coils is a vertical distance of 8 mm and a horizontal offset of 0 mm. The structure should be designed for a vertical distance of 8 mm and a horizontal offset of 0 mm; the deviation range accounts for structural aging deformation and material machining tolerances.
- **Coil Parallelism**: The TX coil must be parallel to the RX coil.
- **Temperature Resistance**: The maximum operating temperature of the TX coil is 105 °C. The structural parts in contact with the TX coil must meet this temperature resistance requirement.

### Hardware Constraints

In high-power charging scenarios, the thermal dissipation related to wireless charging consists of the ACDC thermal dissipation, the overall TX board thermal dissipation, the TX coil thermal dissipation, the RX coil thermal dissipation, and the RX ESC board thermal dissipation. The thermal dissipation of each module is as follows:

| Module | Thermal Dissipation |
|:---|:---|
| Overall TX Board | 32.35 W |
| Overall RX ESC Board | 22.5 W |
| TX Coil | 12 W |
| RX Coil | 5 W |

- At 220 V input, the ACDC has a full-load efficiency of 92% and a maximum output of 800 W. At the maximum wireless charging power, the ACDC provides 380 W to the TX board (charging only, excluding the external circulation fan; the external circulation fan's full-speed power is 20 W, with a startup peak of 72 W). The specific ACDC thermal dissipation must be calculated based on the above information and the overall system power.
- The TX coil has a temperature resistance of 125 °C, and the RX coil has a temperature resistance of 125 °C. The software over-temperature protection value for both is 105 °C. When designing thermal dissipation, reserve a margin of at least 5–10 °C.

## Flight System Requirements

### Takeoff and Landing Wind Speed Limits

The takeoff and landing wind speed limit for the Matrice 4D / 4TD drone is 8 m/s, and the wind speed during operation is limited to below 12 m/s.

### Offset Distance

Non-official docks follow the manual-flight precision landing standard, with a landing accuracy of 30 cm, requiring the drone to return to position normally when offset by 30 cm.

### Marker Requirements

The Marker pattern is mainly black and white, with black as the base color (if the color is limited, a dark shade such as dark gray can also be selected). The reflectance requirements for the black-and-white coating of the Marker landing pad are as follows:

- Reflectance check at the 500 nm visible-light band: black area reflectance < 4%, white area reflectance > 80%.
- Reflectance check at the 450 nm visible-light band: black area < 0.75%, white area > 65%.

The pattern style refers to the DJI Dock 3 landing pad:

- The side length of the isosceles right triangle in the pattern is 100 mm.
- The distance between the left and right edges of the complete white block pattern is 500 mm.
- The relative position of the pattern must not be modified or changed.
- The white pattern must leave a black base-color outer edge of more than 20 mm, i.e., the complete Marker pattern (including the black edge) must be kept above 540 mm.
- Within a radius of at least 8 m around the Marker, patterns extremely similar to the Marker (including identical Marker patterns) are not allowed, otherwise the landing success rate will be affected.

:::warning Note:
Ensure that the Marker remains clearly visible at all times during use, with no abnormal obstruction. When the pattern wear area exceeds 30%, or the pattern is significantly deformed, the pattern coating or landing pad must be updated.
:::
