The O4 Ground Station connects to third-party devices through Ethernet ports. This section introduces the network ports of the O4 Ground Station and their configuration.

## Ethernet Port Description

:::tip Note:
For the first access and debugging, it is recommended to use the LAN port (right). The Broker address is fixed at `192.168.50.10:1883`, with no additional lookup required.
:::

:::warning Warning:
- `192.168.50.11` is a reserved address of the O4 Ground Station; accessing devices must not use this IP.
- Both Ethernet ports support PoE power reception (PD) and can supply power to the Ground Station via a PoE switch; however, the ports do not supply power externally (PSE is not supported), so accessing devices must provide their own power supply.
:::
